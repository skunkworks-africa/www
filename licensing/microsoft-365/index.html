Good to know

Licensing questions

Can I transfer an existing subscription?

Often, but eligibility depends on the vendor, current agreement and account status. We will check the transfer path and any timing or authorisation requirements before proceeding.

Is a monthly payment the same as a monthly commitment?

No. An annual commitment can be billed monthly. Early cancellation and seat reductions depend on the subscription and vendor rules; check the quote before ordering.

Are migration and ongoing support included?

They depend on the agreed scope. Your quote will identify licence charges separately from any migration, setup and support services.

Are volume discounts or promotions available?

We will check current eligibility, quantities, dates and supplier terms when preparing your quote. A discount is only offered when confirmed in writing.
